Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Budgi (“we”, “us”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the Budgi iOS application. Budgi is designed to keep your financial data under your control: budgeting data lives on your device, with optional Apple services you choose to enable.

2. Information Budgi stores

Depending on how you use the app, Budgi may store:

  • Budget categories, spending limits, expenses, income, notes, merchants, and related ledger data
  • Optional receipt images you attach to expenses
  • Optional grocery stores, items, and price entries in the Prices feature
  • Optional recurring transaction templates
  • App preferences such as appearance, accent color, and notification settings
  • On-device Ask Budgi chat history (stored with file protection on your device)

This information is stored locally using Apple’s SwiftData / on-device storage. We do not operate a Budgi backend that collects your budget contents for advertising or resale.

3. Optional Apple services

If you enable them, Budgi may use the following Apple-provided services:

  • iCloud / CloudKit — to sync your Budgi data across your devices and to support optional budget sharing with people you invite
  • Sign in with Apple — to identify your account for sync and sharing features; we store the Apple user identifier and display name you provide in the Keychain / app storage
  • StoreKit — to process Budgi Plus subscriptions; purchase status is managed by Apple
  • FinanceKit (where available) — only if you choose to import transactions from Apple’s finance APIs
  • Camera / Photos — only if you choose to capture or attach receipt images
  • Notifications — optional budget alert notifications you allow
  • On-device Apple Intelligence / Foundation Models — Ask Budgi runs privately on supported devices; prompts and answers are not sent to a Budgi server

Apple’s handling of data for these system services is governed by Apple’s privacy policies and your device settings.

4. Sharing with others

If you create or join a shared budget, the data in that shared group is visible to participants you invite (or who invited you) through CloudKit sharing. Receipt images and transaction details in a shared group may be visible to those participants. You control invitations and can stop sharing from Settings where available.

5. Analytics, advertising, and selling data

Budgi does not sell your personal information. The app is not designed around third-party advertising trackers. We do not use your budget contents to build advertising profiles.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it in-app or delete the app. iCloud-synced data is retained according to your Apple ID / iCloud settings. You can sign out of Sign in with Apple features in Settings, manage subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings, and delete the app to remove local storage.

7. Children’s privacy

Budgi is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. International users

Because Budgi relies on Apple platforms, your data may be processed in locations where Apple operates infrastructure according to your Apple ID region and device configuration.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Budgi after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
